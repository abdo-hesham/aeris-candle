# AERIS

## Register
Brand: a luxury candle campaign and product landing page.

## Users and purpose
People exploring a candle for a calm home ritual. The experience introduces the atmosphere, scent and product, then leads to the existing local checkout preview.

## Brand
Calm, warm, elegant and cinematic. The September fireplace reference supplied by the user is the current visual direction: taupe ceramic, travertine, a lounge and soft firelight. It supersedes the older forest art direction.

## Principles
Match the supplied composition with live typography and independently moving scene layers. Keep motion subtle and content readable. Avoid generic ecommerce grids. Preserve keyboard access and reduced-motion support documented in the existing design system. Product specifications and the local-only checkout come from the existing project data.
